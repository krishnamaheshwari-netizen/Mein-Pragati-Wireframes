Mein Pragati 2.0 · Jan–June 2026 release

The Mein Pragati you know — only faster, clearer, and built for the next million.

Every change in this release starts from a simple promise: preserve what already works for your Sakhis and FOs. The lifecycle, the approval chain, the photo evidence, the language picker, the red brand — all stay. What changes is the friction underneath.

For Sakhis

The frontline cadre. Everything she sees changes the most — but the muscle memory stays. New: a daily action centre, a fixable rejected loop, a simple personal budget tracker, dashboards she can actually read.

Rejected transactions you can see and fix

For Beneficiaries

The most overlooked user in the system — the rural woman whose financial life this is all about. We give her a tangible artefact, a verification trail, and a voice channel for those who can't read.

receipt
From the addendum

Auto-generated receipt after every transaction

When a transaction is approved, the beneficiary gets a receipt — printable A6, SMS link, or IVR audio readout in her language. QR code links to a verification page. Tangible proof of the work done in her name.

Solves: No tangible artefact today
